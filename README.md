# 📊 NovaMed Solutions Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

The **NovaMed Solutions Sales Performance Dashboard** is a comprehensive Power BI project designed to provide actionable insights into pharmaceutical sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, and product performance. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, identify high-performing markets and customers, evaluate product profitability, and support data-driven decision-making.

This project demonstrates practical Business Intelligence (BI) skills including data modeling, DAX calculations, dashboard design, KPI development, and interactive reporting.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to gain a unified view of sales performance across multiple countries, customer segments, products, and demographics. Without a centralized reporting solution, identifying revenue drivers, profitable products, and customer trends becomes difficult.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing an interactive analytical platform that enables users to:

* Monitor overall business performance.
* Identify top-performing markets and customers.
* Analyze revenue by demographic groups.
* Track profitability and product performance.
* Support strategic planning through data-driven insights.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance Overview

This page provides a high-level overview of customer and revenue performance across multiple dimensions.

### Key Metrics

* Total Customers: **200**
* Total Revenue: **$71.3M**
* Average Revenue per Customer: **$356.5K**

### Visualizations Included

* Revenue by Country
* Revenue by Age Category and Gender
* Revenue by Customer Type
* Average Revenue per Customer Segment
* Revenue Distribution by Gender
* Revenue Distribution by Age Group
* Geographic Revenue Analysis
* Interactive Gender Filters
* Country and Customer Type Analysis

### Key Insights

* Canada generated the highest revenue contribution.
* Preferred Customers produced the highest average revenue per customer.
* Customers aged 55+ represented the largest revenue-generating age group.
* Male customers accounted for the highest share of revenue.

---

## 2. Customer and Profitability Analysis

This page focuses on financial performance, customer contribution, and product profitability.

### Key Metrics

* Quantity Sold: **269K Units**
* Cost of Goods Sold: **$12.9M**
* Total Revenue: **$71.3M**
* Profit: **$58.5M**
* Profit Margin: **82%**

### Visualizations Included

* Monthly Revenue and Profit Trends
* Top 5 Customers by Revenue
* Profitability by Drug Name
* Revenue by Drug Name
* Customer Type Analysis
* Country Filters
* Gender Filters
* Product Performance Metrics

### Key Insights

* Doxycycline and Ergocalciferol generated the highest revenue.
* Amoxicillin and Fluticasone contributed significantly to profitability.
* Revenue and profit remained relatively stable throughout the year.
* A small group of customers contributed a significant percentage of overall revenue.
* Strong profit margins indicate efficient operational performance.
